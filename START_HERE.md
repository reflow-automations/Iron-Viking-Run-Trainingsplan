2. Strava on → start recording
3. Say the premise sentence into the camera (script in `content/SCRIPTS.md`)

**During the run:**
- Distance: **1.5–2 km**. That's it. It should feel easy. Walk whenever.
- Stop once at a bar or wall → do the thing → film it
- Talk whenever you have a thought. Rambling is fine, the pipeline cuts it.

## 🧠 If your head is not cooperating

**Headache / bad day / just not feeling it:** go for a walk and film 15 seconds saying exactly that. That still counts as a Day. Honesty is content — "the internet thinks I'm lying" and "I should have kept my mouth shut" are two of gagiegram's best-performing videos.

**Missed several days:** nothing is broken. The day counter is sequential, not calendar-based. You skipped nothing — the next video is just the next number.

**Overwhelmed:** do step 1 and step 3 only. The rest can wait a day.

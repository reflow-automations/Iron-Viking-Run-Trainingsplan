# Scripts — what to actually say

Everything here is English, ready to read out loud. Don't memorize — say it in your own words, but keep the **structure** and keep the premise sentence identical every time.

---

## 0. The premise sentence

> **"Day [X] of training until I can finish a 42 kilometre obstacle race."**

Rules:
- Say it in the **first 2 seconds**, before anything else. No "hey guys", no intro, no context.
- Same words, same order, every video, for 240 videos. Repetition is the brand.
- Day number always first.

---

## 1. Day 1 script (film this one properly)

**Target: 35–45 seconds.** Film it in 4 separate clips so it's easy to redo one bit.

**Clip 1 — the hook (film standing still, before the run):**
> "Day 1 of training until I can finish a 42 kilometre obstacle race.
> A hundred obstacles. Next April. And I can't run."

**Clip 2 — the honest bit (this is the part people follow for):**
> "I'm not a runner. I've never been a runner. I do calisthenics, I do parkour — I can climb things, I can hang, I can pull myself over a wall.
> But running? I hate running. Two kilometres and I'm done."

**Clip 3 — the plan + the stakes:**
> "So here's the plan: every single session goes on this channel. The good ones and the ones where I fall over.
> Today I'm running two kilometres. In April I need to do forty-two — with a hundred obstacles in between."

**Clip 4 — the CTA (film after the run, sweaty, out of breath — that's the point):**
> "This is day 1. This is what I look like, this is what two kilometres does to me.
> Follow, and hold me to it."

**Also film on Day 1 (you'll want these later, they cost 30 seconds now):**
- The "before" body shot — front and side, same spot, same light. You'll re-shoot this monthly.
- The signature shot — 3 sec running toward the camera.
- Your Strava screenshot of run #1.

## 2. The daily short (your 95% format)

Three beats. 20–40 seconds. Never longer.

| Beat | What | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Premise sentence** + one line of drama | 3 sec |
| 2 | **The thing** — the run, the mission, the fail, the wall | 15–30 sec |
| 3 | **One honest line** to close — how it felt, what hurt, what surprised you | 3–5 sec |

**Fill-in version:**
> "Day ___ of training until I can finish a 42 kilometre obstacle race. Today ___________.
> [do the thing, talk while doing it]
> ___________ ."

**Closers that work** (pick one, don't overthink):
- "That's day ___. See you tomorrow."
- "Still can't run. But less bad than yesterday."
- "That hurt more than it should have."
- "Nine months to go."

---

## 3. Hook bank — the line right after "Day X of…"

Steal these. Rotate them. The hook is 80% of whether anyone watches.

**Struggle / honesty**
- "…and today was the worst one so far."
- "…and I nearly didn't go."
- "…and my legs are still broken from Saturday."
- "…and I just got overtaken by a guy walking his dog."

**Drama / curiosity**
- "…and I did something stupid."
- "…and someone in the comments said I'd quit by now."
- "…and I found the perfect wall."
- "…and today I finally did the thing I've been avoiding."

**Progress (the good days)**
- "…and today I ran further than I ever have in my life."
- "…and something clicked."
- "…and for the first time this didn't feel impossible."

**Excuses turned into content** (use these instead of skipping)
- "…and I have a headache, so today is a walk. It still counts."
- "…and I'm on holiday, so today is a mountain."
- "…and it's raining. Going anyway."

**Never open with:** "hey guys", "so today I…", "welcome back", or an apology for not posting.

## 4. Monthly milestone video (the one that grows the channel)

The dailies keep you honest; **these get the views.** One per month, 25–35 seconds, built on gagiegram's exact structure — I broke his 26-second recap down beat by beat:

| Time | Beat | Your version |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | **Time + promise** | "One month ago I promised the internet I'd finish a 42 kilometre obstacle race." |
| 0:03–0:08 | **The goal** | "A hundred obstacles. And I couldn't run two kilometres." |
| 0:08–0:12 | **Conflict + self-deprecation** | "I had no idea what I was doing. So I did something pretty stupid — I told everyone." |
| 0:12–0:15 | **The premise** | "Every session goes online until race day." |
| 0:15–0:20 | **PROOF** ← the climax | Stats overlay (total km, longest run, pull-ups) + before/after side by side |
| 0:21–0:26 | **Re-hook + CTA** | "I'm nowhere near ready. Follow and watch me suffer." |

**Craft notes (copied from what works):**
- 15+ cuts in 26 seconds. Fast.
- Rapid-cut through weather, outfits, seasons — this is why you film the signature shot daily. Snow, rain, sun, dark: the montage *is* the proof.
- Pop-on captions: one bold word at a time, centred, white with a dark outline.
- Look tired in the footage. Exhaustion reads as honest.

**Milestone moments to build a video around:** first 5K ever · first month · first 10K · the first time you fail an obstacle on camera · vacation hike week · first 20K · 100 days · shoes destroyed · one month out · race week.

---

## 5. Talking-to-camera rules

1. **Say the number first.** Always.
2. **One idea per video.** Not three.
3. **Say the real thing.** "I feel like shit and I'm going anyway" beats any motivational line.
4. **Never apologise** for gaps, low effort, or slow times. Just say the number and keep going.
5. **Ask a question sometimes** — "what should I carry next week?" Comments feed the algorithm and give you ideas for free.
6. Wind noise: talk close, turn your back to the wind, keep going. Don't re-film.
7. If a take is bad — leave it in. The pipeline will pick. **Never re-shoot mid-run.**

The script

Say this first — every video, same words Day X of training until I can finish a 42 kilometre obstacle race.

First two seconds. No “hey guys”, no intro, no context. Day number always first.

Day 1 in four separate clips, so you can redo one bit without redoing all of it. Target 35–45 seconds total.

Clip 1The hook — standing still, before the run
“Day 1 of training until I can finish a 42 kilometre obstacle race. A hundred obstacles. Next April. And I can't run.”
Clip 2The honest bit — this is what people follow for
“I'm not a runner. I've never been a runner. I do calisthenics, I do parkour — I can climb things, I can hang, I can pull myself over a wall. But running? I hate running. Two kilometres and I'm done.”
Clip 3The plan and the stakes
“So here's the plan: every single session goes on this channel. The good ones and the ones where I fall over. Today I'm running two kilometres. In April I need to do forty-two — with a hundred obstacles in between.”
Clip 4The CTA — after the run, sweaty, out of breath
“This is day 1. This is what I look like, this is what two kilometres does to me. Follow, and hold me to it.”
Also film today — costs 30 seconds, worth months

The “before” body shot, front and side, same spot and light (you'll re-shoot this monthly) · the signature shot · your Strava screenshot from run #1.

Every day after

Three beats, 20–40 seconds, never longer.

LengthBeat
3 secPremise sentence + one line of drama
15–30 secThe thing — the run, the mission, the fail, the wall
3–5 secOne honest line — how it felt, what hurt, what surprised you
Hook bank — the line right after “Day X of…”
Struggle
  • “…and today was the worst one so far.”
  • “…and I nearly didn't go.”
  • “…and I just got overtaken by a guy walking his dog.”
Drama
  • “…and I did something stupid.”
  • “…and someone in the comments said I'd have quit by now.”
  • “…and I found the perfect wall.”
Progress
  • “…and today I ran further than I ever have in my life.”
  • “…and something clicked.”
Excuses, turned into content
  • “…and I have a headache, so today is a walk. It still counts.”
  • “…and I'm on holiday, so today is a mountain.”
  • “…and it's raining. Going anyway.”

Never open with: “hey guys”, “so today I…”, “welcome back”, or an apology for not posting.

Monthly milestone video — the one that actually grows the channel

The dailies keep you honest; these get the views. One a month, 25–35 seconds, built on gagiegram's structure.

0:00–0:03“One month ago I promised the internet I'd finish a 42 kilometre obstacle race.”
0:03–0:08“A hundred obstacles. And I couldn't run two kilometres.”
0:08–0:12“I had no idea what I was doing. So I did something pretty stupid — I told everyone.”
0:12–0:15“Every session goes online until race day.”
0:15–0:20Proof — stats overlay (total km, longest run, pull-ups) + before/after side by side
0:21–0:26“I'm nowhere near ready. Follow and watch me suffer.”

15+ cuts in 26 seconds. Rapid-cut through weather and outfits — that's what the daily signature shot is for. Pop-on captions, one bold word at a time, centred. Looking tired reads as honest.

When your head isn't cooperating

Headache, bad day, just not feeling it Go for a walk and film 15 seconds saying exactly that. It still counts as a Day. “The internet thinks I'm lying” and “I should have kept my mouth shut” are two of gagiegram's best-performing videos — honesty is the content.
Missed several days Nothing is broken. The counter is sequential, not calendar-based — you skipped nothing, the next video is just the next number.
Overwhelmed by the list Do step 1 and step 3. The rest can wait a day.
